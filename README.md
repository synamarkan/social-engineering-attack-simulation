# Social Engineering Attack Simulation & Defense Strategy

## Problem Statement
Human error remains one of the weakest links in cybersecurity defenses, making individuals vulnerable to social engineering attacks.

## Objective
To simulate social engineering scenarios in a controlled environment and propose effective mitigation strategies.

## Tools Used
- Kali Linux
- Social-Engineer Toolkit (SET)

## Project Tasks
- Study common social engineering attack techniques.
- Simulate phishing scenarios in an authorized, controlled environment.
- Analyze factors that may contribute to successful attacks.
- Develop security awareness and defense strategies.

## Security Techniques
- Social engineering analysis
- Controlled attack simulation
- Phishing awareness and prevention

## Mitigation Strategies
- Conduct regular cybersecurity awareness training.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Verify suspicious links, emails, and requests.
- Establish clear procedures for reporting suspected phishing attempts.

## Outcome
Developed an understanding of human-centric security vulnerabilities, social engineering risks, and practical defense strategies.

## Ethical Considerations
All simulations must be performed in an authorized lab environment. No real credentials or personal information should be collected.
